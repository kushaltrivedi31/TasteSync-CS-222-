# TasteSync-CS-222-

## Project Overview
TasteSync is a web-based application that gives food recommendations based on the music a user listens to, aka their mood. This app aims to make the process of choosing what to eat more interesting by integrating food discovery with something that everyone consumes daily: music. A user can link their Spotify account or choose a mood manually. TasteSync then analyzes the mood of the playlist and gives food, restaurant, or recipe suggestions based on the analysis. The primary audience of this application are college students and young people who want to try out different foods but cannot decide on what to eat!
